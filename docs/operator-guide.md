# Operator guide — SLB

Portal: no bespoke government-built e-procurement self-service portal
or live Ministry of Finance and Treasury site could be confirmed
reachable this session. Market-entry registration itself runs through
the live `solomonbusinessregistry.gov.sb` electronic register
(Companies, Business Names, Foreign Investors, About Solomon Islands).
Entity: Company Haus (Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Commerce,
Industries, Labour and Immigration/MCILI, Companies Act 2009) or
Business Names Act 2014 registration + Inland Revenue Division (IRD)
TIN record (Income Tax Act, Cap 123, via TAA 1 / TAA 1A / E-Tax).
Engagements that rely on an existing Foreign Investment Act 2005
Certificate of Registration to cover a NEW investment activity also
clear the certificate's own activity-scope eligibility gate (s.21(2)(b))
before a filing can proceed. Human-gated filing only.
