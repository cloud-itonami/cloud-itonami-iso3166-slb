# Business model — SLB

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for Solomon Islands.

- Public Financial Management Act 2013 (No. 9 of 2013), Part 9 (ss.72-
  75): the Minister of Finance prescribes procurement rules/
  regulations; accountable/accounting officers must follow prescribed
  procurement planning, tendering and contracting processes; s.73(2)(d)
  prohibits apportioning a procurement transaction into parts to avoid
  the procurement rules (see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`). No live
  Ministry of Finance and Treasury site could be reached this session,
  and the delegated procurement Regulations were not found/fetched --
  honestly, no specific procurement-value dollar threshold is modelled.
- Company Haus (Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Commerce,
  Industries, Labour and Immigration/MCILI) company registration under
  the Companies Act 2009 (No. 1 of 2009), and Business Names Act 2014
  (No. 13 of 2014) business-name registration, both through the live
  `solomonbusinessregistry.gov.sb` electronic register
- Registrar of Foreign Investment (InvestSolomons/Foreign Investment
  Division, MCILI) Certificate of Registration under the Foreign
  Investment Act 2005 (No. 7 of 2005)
- Inland Revenue Division (IRD) TIN (Tax Identification Number)
  registration via forms TAA 1 / TAA 1A and E-Tax -- compulsory for the
  legal entity operating a business, per IRD's own tax-information page
- Foreign Investment Act 2005 ss.12(3)-(4)/13(a)/21(2)(b) certificate-
  scope gate -- bars a filing that claims an existing Certificate of
  Registration already covers the investment activity it is now
  conducting, when that activity is not actually among the specific
  activities named on the certificate itself
